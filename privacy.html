Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

DyslexiaBuddy is made by Onlabs Infotech ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the DyslexiaBuddy app (on iPhone, iPad and Mac, plus its App Clip and widgets) and this website collect, and what we do with it.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email or age.
  • What you read stays on your device. Your documents, scans, photos, highlights, notes, flashcards and tutor chats are not sent to us.
  • Voices and text recognition run on your device.
  • We collect anonymous usage data and crash reports so we can see which features help and fix bugs.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell data.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you bring into the app is stored on your device, in the app's own storage. That includes:

  • Documents, PDFs and images you import, and the text the app reads from them
  • Pages you scan with the camera
  • Highlights, notes, flashcards and your reading progress
  • Conversations with the reading tutor
  • Your settings, such as font, colors and voice

When you scan a page or open a photo, the app finds the words using Apple's built-in text recognition, which runs on your device. The reading voices also run on your device, so listening works without the internet. The voice files themselves are downloaded from Apple when you first use them.

The free books in the Library come inside the app. Opening one does not contact any server.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: only when you choose to scan a page or read with the camera. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Photos: the app uses Apple's photo picker, so it only sees the photos you pick.
  • Files: the app only opens the files you choose to import.
  • Notifications: optional. If you turn on reading reminders, the app schedules them on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device's Settings.

The app does not use your microphone and does not record your voice.

3. The reading tutor and Word Card

The tutor, word explanations and Word Card pictures use Apple Intelligence on devices that support it. Most requests run on your device. Some longer requests may use Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designed so that your data is used only for your request and is not stored or seen by Apple. In both cases, the text you ask about is not sent to us or to any other AI company.

4. Anonymous usage data and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics in the app. We use the version of Firebase Analytics that does not use the advertising identifier.

This data helps us understand things like how many people finish setup, which features get used, and where the app crashes. It may include:

  • Actions in the app, such as "started listening", "imported a PDF" or "opened a free book"
  • Your answers to the optional questions at the start (for example, who the app is for, how you heard about it, and what feels hard about reading)
  • The word you tap when you ask the app to define a single word
  • Device type, operating system and app version, and approximate country
  • A random ID for this install of the app, which is not linked to your name or email
  • Crash and performance reports

We do not collect the text of your documents, your notes, your tutor conversations or your photos.

The app also registers with Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives your device a random token so the app can receive notifications. This token is not linked to who you are.

5. Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription or trial is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt from Apple and a random app user ID, not your name or email.

6. This website

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects details such as the pages you view, your browser and device type, and your approximate location. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may log basic request details (such as IP address) to keep the site secure and running.

7. Advertising and tracking

DyslexiaBuddy has no ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use your data for advertising.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your data. The only companies that receive the data described above are the service providers named in this policy (Google Firebase, Google Analytics, RevenueCat, Cloudflare and Apple), and only to provide their service to us.

9. Children

Many people who use DyslexiaBuddy are children, often with a parent's help. The app does not ask for a name, email, age or any other contact details, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The usage data described above is anonymous and is used only to improve the app. If you are a parent and have a question about your child's data, please email us.

10. How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app. Anonymous usage data and crash reports are kept by Google for a limited time and then deleted automatically.

11. Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Turn off notifications in the app or in your device's Settings.
  • Email us if you want to ask about, or ask us to delete, data related to your use of the app.

12. Links to other websites

The app and this website link to a few other sites, such as the App Store and research articles. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
