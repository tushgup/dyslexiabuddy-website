What's New in DyslexiaBuddy 6.2: Free Books and More Voices

September 27, 2026 4 min read

Sometimes the hardest part of reading practice is finding something good to read. DyslexiaBuddy 6.2 puts a shelf of classic stories inside the app, opens up every English voice on your device, and makes the reader calmer on iPad and Mac.

A free Library of classic books

The new Library tab has 14 public-domain children's books, each with its real first-edition cover. Every book opens in the same reader as your scans and PDFs, so the fonts, colors, spacing, read-aloud, and tutor all work the same way.

The shelf is sorted by reading level, and you can filter it by topic: Animals, Adventure, Magic, Fairy tales, Friendship, and Funny.

  • First stories, ages 5 to 7: The Tale of Peter Rabbit, The Tale of Benjamin Bunny, The Tale of Squirrel Nutkin, The Tale of Tom Kitten, The Tale of Jemima Puddle-Duck, and The Velveteen Rabbit.
  • Chapter books, ages 8 to 10: Alice's Adventures in Wonderland, The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, The Adventures of Pinocchio, and Grimms' Fairy Tales.
  • Classic novels, ages 10 and up: The Wind in the Willows, Black Beauty, Treasure Island, and Anne of Green Gables.

Chosen with care

The books come from Project Gutenberg and are edited for easier reading, with chapters you can jump between. They are part of the app, so they work offline. We left out some famous titles because they contain racist language or stories that we do not want to put in front of young readers.

Every English voice on your device

Before, the voice list showed only a short list of names. Now it shows every English voice on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac, with the best quality first. That includes the Enhanced and Premium voices that Apple gives free.

If you do not have these voices yet, a new Download more voices guide in Settings shows the steps, with a short video. When you come back, the new voice is already in the list.

Premium voices are easier to try, too. If you listen to a premium voice and then subscribe, that voice stays selected, so you can continue reading at once.

A calmer reader on iPad and Mac

On iPad, the top bar of the reader now has fewer buttons. The actions you use less often are in the ellipsis menu, so more of the screen is for the text. DyslexiaBuddy 6.2 is also available on the Mac, with the same reader, voices, and Library.

See what is new in the app

After you update, a short What's New screen shows these changes one time, with a button that opens the Library. Pick a book that fits the reader, change the page until it feels comfortable, and press play.
