Questions

Is DyslexiaBuddy free?

Yes. Scanning, read-aloud with your device's voices, word meanings, and the Library are free.

Can I try Premium first?

Yes. Every feature, including the AI voices and Olly, is free for 7 days. Cancel in your Apple account settings before the week ends and you pay nothing.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning, read-aloud, and word meanings work without an internet connection.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone, iPad, and Mac, on iOS 26 or later. Olly needs a device with Apple Intelligence.

Is it only for children?

No. Adults use it for work documents and study material too.
