See what changes when reading gets hard.

A short tour through the real app—from the page in front of you to the help you need next.

  1. 1.0

    Make the page feel readable.

    Change the font, colors, text size, line height, and letter spacing while previewing every choice. Switch reading surfaces without leaving the page, and choose from familiar and dyslexia-friendly fonts.

  2. 2.0

    Choose a voice, then follow along.

    Pick an accent and voice, set the pace, and follow the highlighted text while DyslexiaBuddy reads aloud. Six accents are included in the free experience, while premium adds 28 human-sounding voices.

  3. 3.0

    Ask for a clearer starting point.

    Turn a long document into a short summary, or ask the reading tutor a question without leaving the passage. Summaries surface the main ideas in simpler points, and the tutor stays alongside the imported document.

  4. 4.0

    Practice the words that slowed you down.

    Return to unfamiliar words as simple review cards, check the definition, and mark what needs another pass. Saved words become a focused practice set, and readers decide what they know and what to revisit.

Questions parents and educators ask first.

Plain answers before anyone downloads.

Is DyslexiaBuddy only for children?

No. The reading tools can also support adults working through reports, documents, study material, or books.

Can the app work offline?

Yes. The App Store listing says scanning, reading tools, and on-device word definitions can work without an internet connection.

Which devices are supported?

DyslexiaBuddy is available for iPhone and iPad. Check the App Store listing for current operating-system requirements.

Does one reading setup work for everyone?

No. Readers can adjust fonts, colors, line spacing, text size, voice, and speed to find a setup that works better for them.
