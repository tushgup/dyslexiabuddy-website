Questions from parents and teachers

Is DyslexiaBuddy only for children?

No. Adults use it for work documents and study material too.

Can the app work offline?

Yes. Scanning, read-aloud, and word meanings work without an internet connection.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone, iPad, and Mac, on iOS 26 or later. Olly needs a device with Apple Intelligence.

Are there books to read in the app?

Yes. The Library tab groups its free books by age, from first stories for ages 5 to 7 to novels for ages 10 and up.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Premium is free for 7 days, and you can cancel in your Apple account settings.
